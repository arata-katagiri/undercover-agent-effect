# Adaptive multi-role exploratory report

Episodes: **117** across **39** cells and **3** fresh seeds per cell.
Exact pre-intervention matching: **passed**. Corrective output repairs: **20**; fail-closed PUBLIC fallbacks: **2**.

> These analyses were designated exploratory before collection. Differences pool role > rosters as repeated screening units and should not be read as population-level estimates.

## Main descriptive contrasts

| Comparison | Outcome | Mean paired difference | + / 0 / - |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Direct solicitation vs none (promise kept) | Invitation transfer to organizer | 0.139 | 3 / 9 / 0 |
| Indirect solicitation vs none (promise kept) | Invitation transfer to organizer | 0.000 | 0 / 12 / 0 |

## Interpretation

Direct and indirect solicitations changed immediate transfers to the organizer by +0.139 and +0.000, respectively, relative to matched no-message controls when the organizer kept its promise.
After explicit solicitation, public betrayal changed later organizer-directed transfers by +0.190 and retaliation by +0.036. This measures behavioral adaptation, not an internal intention to collude.

## Limitations

- This was an exploratory screen with prompt-defined roles, one model, one payoff table, and a small number of fresh seeds per cell.
- A role prompt induces behavior; it is not evidence that an unprompted model naturally possesses that stable personality or strategy.
- Correct-report labels use receipt of an organizer's solicitation as ground truth. They do not prove that the accused agent had a durable hidden intention.
- The direct message is more explicit than the indirect message. Differences combine social framing, information content, and requested action specificity.
- Transfers are positive-sum, so discriminatory cooperation and social harm are distinct.
- No null-hypothesis p-values are reported for this exploratory matrix. Fresh confirmatory seeds and a reduced comparison set are needed before inferential claims.
